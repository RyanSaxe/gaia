# Every trail Jev wants is walked, on one network of paths

Every dependency Jev would walk is routed, on one network of paths that
trails share. A trail joins the paths earlier ones made unless that is
about twice as far, and new tread keeps clear of existing tread, so
trails merge into trunks and branch at junctions instead of running side
by side. The network, not a count of trails, holds the composition: its
treads cover at most 5% of any region's ground and 2% of the land, and a
trail that doesn't fit, or that no gentle, dry route reaches, is dropped
with a reason. A shared stretch is worn by every trail on it, so it stays
trodden while any of them thrives and grows over only when all of them
fail. A small cairn stands where three or more ways meet away from a
place.

routes only 5 of the 20 chosen. — These are important to address."
