# Jev generates the world

Jev generates components and worlds by filling strongly typed schemas, chained across many decisions. Most of the world is judgment, not bookkeeping: Jev, not hard-coded rules, decides which kind represents a piece of code, when a dependency becomes visible, and what is worth telling the person. The schema offers options instead of fixed mappings, and the world must not be dominated by paths.
