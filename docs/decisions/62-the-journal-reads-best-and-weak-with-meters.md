# The journal reads best and weak, with a meter for each number

A journal page gives quality and importance each as a percentage after a
short meter, quality's in its health color and importance's in ink, beside
the name and on every row. A marked block keeps its brush stroke, now to
the left of the handwritten line numbers, and is labeled **best**, **weak**
or **key** in a column of its own. A page's weak part is chosen first; its
best is the code that runs, never a type, that scores above the page
itself, by quality weighed by importance, so a page may have none.

## Why

The reviewer, after the merge: "I feel like the quality and importance
numbers are weird where quality gets a color and importance doesn't. And
importance isn't shown. And then like these lines for line numbers look off
and so does like the way we consider the good exemplary block or the bad
block." On Gaia's world a page's best had been an interface or a type in 73
of 168 files. On the round's polish page the reviewer chose a meter for
both numbers, today's line numbers, a labeled margin stroke and the best
among code that beats the page; in its thread, "I like best and weak the
most".
