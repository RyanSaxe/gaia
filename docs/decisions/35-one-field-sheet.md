# One field sheet: the map, the minimap and what a thing says

The field map, the minimap, a thing's sketch page and the wait are one
sheet of the traveller's paper, painted and lettered in one language
(`docs/design-system.md`, "The field sheet"). The map is a painted
topographic map: washes in each area's land color that wilt with its
health, hills shaded and lit, and sepia contours from the real heights,
with its names, trails, houses and vignettes part of the painting and a
legend of the land's health. The slip at the lower left is replaced by a
minimap: a torn scrap of the same map showing the land around the person,
north up, with the area's name lettered on it, changing cleanly as they
cross into another area; a tap unfolds the field map out of it, and close
in the map shows each file's patch and name. Nothing in the world lights up
under the pointer: the rim of light is gone. Selecting shows on paper: a
tap on a thing walks the person up to it, the minimap rings it in
vermilion pencil, and on arrival the minimap grows into the thing's sketch
page, where the sketch is the news and its notes say only what is specific
and true of the thing. While the world is judged, a brush works over the
waiting sheet where questions are out, over clear water laid there, so the
wait shows it is still drawing through every lull. Areas' and the world's
vitality pool their files' by size (`areaVitality`, `groundVitality` in
`@gaia/world`).

## Why

After round 15 the reviewer asked for "a mix between the topography one and
painted", coloring that is "not just real, but reflective of vitality", and
for the map, the slip and what a thing says to "belong to a singular and
clean design system" that the world feels part of: "Bottom left can be
where you are in the world as a minimap with a label. Clicking opens the
full map." The minimap "isn't to show the whole area. It is really to show
the local area", and the map "can expand to show a lower level entity". A
highlighted tree "breaks immersion". Of what a thing says: "the sketch is
the news for the open variant", but "they all say the same stuff about
tests and lines ... It feels fake." The round-16 drawing board recommended
the brush at work with wetting the paper for the wait's lulls.

## Settled

Pair session `gaia-v3` (`f8d282bc-7712-4894-9255-dcf4699f92cb`), round 16
(the wayfinding page), building on round 15's map, paper, vitality and
drawing boards. It supersedes the slip, the line and the rim of
[decision 32](32-a-slip-ask-and-a-rim.md).

Refined by [42](42-the-field-map-shows-health.md): color on the field map is health alone, with no legend, title, region names, fringe or turned-down corner.
