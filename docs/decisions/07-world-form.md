# A beautiful world you wander through

Gaia v3 is a beautiful world a person wanders at eye height, with no avatar, and can zoom all the way out to see whole. Terrain has gentle landforms within a relief budget (24 m range, at least 95% walkable), and none of v1's or v2's mountains. The edge is a gentle rim, and the world should seem infinite: distant land dims to a silhouette that bleeds into the sky's own color instead of a separate fog band. The person can wade but never goes under water, grass must never look as if it grows out of the ground as the person approaches, and night must stay readable.

## Why
