# Quality is the reviewer's ten concepts, and vitality is quality alone

Quality is ten concepts: correctness, readability, simplicity, cohesion,
loose coupling, explicitness, consistency, testability, robustness and
efficiency. Jev scores every one; none comes from the graph. Robustness
also has "nothing here can fail", which doesn't count. A definition is
asked all ten, a file six and a folder three
(`engine/vocab/understanding.toml`). Vitality in the world is that
quality alone: a file's and a definition's are their own, and an entity's
is its folder's.

kept one 95% of the time (round 14, concepts-test page). Testability came
out reversed there, since kata code is plain logic, and the reviewer kept
it as it is. Vitality from quality alone was the reviewer's choice on
