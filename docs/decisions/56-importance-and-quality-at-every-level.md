# Importance and quality at every level, judged top-down with the whole project in view

Every folder, file and definition has two axes, importance and quality,
and they are the main metadata the journal shows. Understanding runs
top-down: each folder is asked after its parent, and each file after its
folder, so every request carries the project's own words and what was
found above it. Jev judges importance at each level on its own, in the
light of the whole project, and there is no arithmetic between levels: a
function Jev calls The heart is 1, whatever its folder got. Quality is
local, and the engine gathers it upward by lines. Importance never feeds
vitality; it shows through what Jev composes.

## Why

Importance judged as a share of its container made a one-block function's
block look important, and a part could never matter more than what holds
it. The reviewer: "there is still a notion of importance at a lower
level. so lets be careful. I think the point here is your architecture
should cascade context!!!", and on capping a part at its container: "What
if a particular function is like THE HEART." Importance "isn't like
vitality where the world scales things up. It should help jev choose what
to represent something as."
